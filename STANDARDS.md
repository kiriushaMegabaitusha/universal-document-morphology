# The standardised landscape — an inventory

Four layers, from the invisible substrate up to the theory. Sources are keyed
to `ledger.json`.

## Layer 0 — the invisible substrate (genuinely universal)

Not thought of as "document formatting", and for that reason almost never
mentioned in national rules such as Постанова КМУ №55, yet universal:

- ISO 216 — paper sizes (A4).
- ISO 8601 — dates and times.
- ISO 3166 — country codes.
- ISO 639 — language codes.
- ISO 4217 — currency codes.

A Ukrainian наказ, a Chinese 公文 and a US Federal Register rule all stand on
this layer.

## Layer 1 — micro-layout standards (national, mutually incompatible)

The visible "tangled mess". These fix *how the page looks*.

| standard | jurisdiction | what it fixes |
|----------|--------------|---------------|
| ДСТУ 4163:2020 | Ukraine | 32 requisites (01–32) for organisational-administrative documents; in force 2021-09-01, replacing ДСТУ 4163:2003 [S-DSTU2020] |
| ГОСТ Р 7.0.97-2016 | RU / CIS | the sibling standard, in force 2017-07-01; shares the ЕГСД ancestor [S-GOSTR, S-ECSD] |
| DIN 5008:2020 | Germany | 9-component business-letter layout; text-processing rules; revised since 1949 [S-DIN5008] |
| GB/T 9704-2012 | China | head 版头 / body 主体 / record 版记 layout for Party and government documents [S-GBT9704] |
| ISO 8439 | international | forms design |
| ÖNORM A 1080 | Austria | business-letter layout |
| JIS Z 8301 etc. | Japan | business-document layout |

The companion *workflow* rules (who registers, when, where a mark goes) are a
separate instrument: in Ukraine, Постанова КМУ №55 of 2018 approves the Типова
інструкція з діловодства; ДСТУ 4163 defines the requisites, the decree defines
the movement.

## Layer 2 — records-management standards (international, but abstract)

Govern how a record lives and dies; say almost nothing about the page.

- ISO 15489-1:2016 — records management, concepts and principles [S-ISO15489].
- ISO 16175-2:2011 — functional requirements for electronic records systems
  [S-ISO16175].
- MoReq2010 — Modular Requirements for Records Systems, the EU model
  specification [S-MOREQ].
- ISO 23081 — records metadata; ISO 30301 — records management systems.

## Layer 3 — institutional discourse regimes

- **UN** — every document carries a unique symbol whose components encode the
  issuing organ and its subsidiary bodies [S-UNSYM]; the Editorial Manual
  fixes the resolution's order of elements as name of organ → preamble →
  operative part [S-UNRES].
- **EU** — the Interinstitutional Style Guide fixes the legal act as title →
  preamble (citations + recitals) → articles (enacting terms) → annexes
  [S-EUISG].
- National drafting manuals: the US Federal Register Document Drafting
  Handbook and Plain Writing Act; the UK Government Digital Service content
  design standards; Brazil's Manual de Redação.

## Layer 4 — the theory

- **Diplomatics** — Mabillon, *De re diplomatica* (1681), refounded by
  Luciana Duranti as archival diplomatics: the document's inner constitution
  as act, persons, procedure, form [S-DIPLOMATICS].
- **Records continuum** — Frank Upward's time/space model, versus the Anglo
  lifecycle model [S-CONTINUUM].
- **Digital turn** — ISO 24495-1:2023 (plain language) [S-ISO24495]; Rules as
  Code / digital-ready legislation [S-EIF-RAC].

## The reading of the landscape

Convergent at the depth (the substrate plus a common document grammar),
divergent at the surface (layout, wording rituals), and split across four
professional communities — records managers, standardisers, legal drafters and
plain-language people — that rarely cite one another. See `morphology.md` §5
for what follows from this.
