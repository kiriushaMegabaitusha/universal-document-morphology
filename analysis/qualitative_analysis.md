## 3. Onomastics (now 28 named types across 11 systems, `document_types.csv`)

Four naming logics persist — by authority (Saudi), by function (Indonesia),
by ritual role (Francophone), by format (China) — and the Soviet/CIS gap is
now **closed**, adding a fifth:

- **By documentary function (Soviet/CIS):** Ukrainian organisational-
  administrative documentation is classified into **organisational** (статут,
  положення, інструкція, договір), **dispositive** (наказ, вказівка, ухвала,
  розпорядження, рішення) and **reference-informational** (акт, протокол,
  довідка, лист) groups, and the national classifier НК 010:2021 — the
  successor to the all-Union ОКУД and to ДК 010-98 — enumerates the individual
  types (акт, наказ, протокол, довідка, статут, положення, договір, …)
  [S-SU-NK010, S-SU-ORD].

So "what kind of document is this?" is answered by **rank** in one tradition,
by **job** in another, by **social act** in a third, by **layout** in a fourth,
and by **documentary function** in the Soviet/CIS one. The 28-type lexicon now
draws on 11 systems; the naming logics are culturally organised and show no
single shared vocabulary.

Readings hold and sharpen with the Soviet bloc (placed at 1973, in the middle
band):

- **The identifier (S2) is the modern slot** — 4/10 to 7/9 across the last
  century; the bloc's ОКУД/ГОСТ contribute to the mid-century rise of
  classification.
- **Authentication is the persistent slot** — 8/10 in the mid-century band, the
  era that added the bloc's endorsement griffs.
- **Digital signature** (S12 re-encoded) appears only after 2015 (AE, BJ, BR,
  EE, ID, IN, SA, VN).
- The **preamble (S9)** stays confined to the Roman-canon normative tradition
  (EU, UN) and never enters national page-standards or the bloc.

## 5. Synthesis

- Fork = formal negation (S9 ⊥ the correspondence furniture).
- Universal core = authentication + custody (theme coding), matching the slot
  census (S12 = 17/25).
- Genre is culturally *named*, not universally categorised.
- Modernity = the identifier; authentication is the migrating slot; the
  preamble is a normative-tradition relic.

## 6. Method caveats

Keyword theme coding (not double-coded; no kappa). Era dating is a regime anchor
year with small bins (n = 6–10) — directional, not probative. Implication rules
inherit coding sparsity (the JP=KE=HN 1.0 artifact). Onomastics is complete for
the 11 systems that name document types; the other 17 systems contribute no
named type, so the lexicon is bounded by the corpus, not by the field.
