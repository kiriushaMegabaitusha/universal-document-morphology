## P09-1 — Validity, not reliability: can the 14-slot deep grammar be re-induced from the requisites themselves?

**Participant: P09 | Stage: post-check | Origin: AI-generated | Date: 2026-10-15**

**Statement (idea).** Freeze the slot vocabulary and all text preprocessing, cluster the 225 requisites' own descriptions (`local_term` + `gloss_en` in `requisites.csv`) unsupervised over a pre-registered range of k, align the recovered clusters to S1–S14, and report ARI/NMI against a fixed-margin permutation null — asking whether the atlas's deep grammar re-emerges from the data or exists only in the analyst's codebook.

**Mechanism/rationale (idea).** The evidence wave closed the reliability route: a machine second coder (P02-1) and blind re-coding of four layers (P06-1) are challenge-located prior art, and E01 additionally recorded that LLM-vs-human annotation in a large empirical evaluation ran at Krippendorff α 0.12–0.46 with downstream conclusions changing in over a third of cases — so an agreement coefficient can show that a vocabulary is *repeatable*, never that it is *right*. Validity is the untested question, and the instrument for it (TF–IDF/character-n-gram clustering + Hungarian label alignment + permutation null) is textbook on purpose: the contribution is the object — the first recoverability test of an official-document slot ontology — not the method. The design also re-tests finding 2 out-of-sample for free: if the S6/S9 declension split reappears as two stable cluster branches without ever being coded, that is corroboration of the fork by a route that cannot inherit the fork.

**Assumptions.**
- *assumption:* `gloss_en` is a faithful and comparably phrased rendering of the local terms, so lexical similarity tracks functional similarity rather than translation style.
- *assumption:* 225 short strings carry enough signal to cluster at all; vocabulary (tokens, stopwords, n-gram range, linkage, k grid) is frozen in a config file **before** any cluster is inspected, per the round's revise disposition.
- *assumption:* the permutation null preserves slot-size margins (and a second null preserves system margins) so that chance agreement at an unbalanced 14-way partition is benchmarked correctly.
- *assumption:* the same analyst wrote both glosses and slot labels (same-source circularity); this cannot be fully removed without a second coder — whose value the evidence wave already discounted — so it is handled as an explicit sensitivity, not silently.

**Predicted observations.**
- *prediction:* ARI/NMI sits clearly above both nulls at the frozen k, with the stem slots (S1, S5, S10, S12) forming a stable core of clusters across resamples.
- *prediction:* the two declensions separate as distinct branches (S6-group vs S9-group) without being seeded, and the sparse slots (S11, S9) are the ones that float between clusters.
- *prediction:* the full ARI-vs-k curve is monotone-ish rather than sharply peaked at 14 — recoverability is graded, and the reportable number is the curve, not one tuned figure.

**Disconfirming observation (prediction).** Disconfirmed if ARI/NMI is statistically indistinguishable from the fixed-margin null at every frozen k (the grammar is an imposed classification — after which the coder-audit cluster P02-1/P06-1 can only ever have measured repeatability of an arbitrary scheme), or if stable structure appears only at k ≪ 14 (the vocabulary over-segments, so the "universal stem" is a coarser construct than claimed).

**Main feasibility risk (assumption).** Lexical signal may be too weak: glosses are terse, multilingual and partly duplicated, and cluster count plus preprocessing are researcher choices with real degrees of freedom — mitigated only by freezing them pre-computation and publishing the whole k/preprocessing grid, not by post-hoc justification.

**Repo connections (fact).** `requisites.csv` (225 rows; `local_term`, `gloss_en`, `slot`, `system`, `obligation`); `analysis/slot_matrix.csv`; `analysis/recompute.py`; `check_claims.py` (which already enforces the closed slot vocabulary); `morphology.md` §1 (the 14 slots and the two declensions).

**responds to:** challenge-located P02-1 and P06-1 (second-coder/κ machinery located as prior art *and* empirically unreliable per E01) plus the revise disposition "freeze definitions/nulls/coding rules pre-computation" — the pivot converts the coder-audit cluster from reliability to validity, where the application (a documentary-morphology ontology) is itself the contribution.

## P09-2 — Independent invention or inherited form: a descent-controlled convergence test

**Participant: P09 | Stage: post-check | Origin: AI-generated | Date: 2026-10-15**

**Statement (idea).** Build a frozen descent/citation edge set over the atlas's own standards and administrations from documents already on disk (`STANDARDS.md`, `diversity.md`, `cases/`, `ledger.json` — no new retrieval), and re-test the convergence claims (universal stem, S6⊥S9, "genre, not nation") under lineage-blocked nulls plus a profile-similarity regression on descent distance, partitioning the "deep grammar" into convergent structure versus common descent.

**Mechanism/rationale (idea).** E02's own evidence wave located the instrument: co-occurrence inflation from shared history/contact (Galton's problem) and area/genera-blocked conditional permutation are established prior art in the nearest domain (Dryer's WALS critique; the Bickel-group randomization-test line) — so the method is adopted openly, not claimed, and the whole burden of novelty moves to the application: nobody has ever blocked a document-format typology for descent. This is the synthesis the matrix lacks: P06-3 partitions genre vs tradition family but never models genealogical edges, P08-3 codes only a single colonial-heritage category, P07-2 hashes document *texts* for template duplication rather than institutions for descent, and P04-3 (edition diffing) is paused for retrieval/licence — edges extracted from on-disk files sidestep that pause entirely. The payoff is a substantive re-read of findings 1–3: if similarity decays with descent distance, part of "universal" is inheritance, while a declension effect that survives blocking is evidence of independent reinvention.

**Assumptions.**
- *assumption:* documented relations exist in the frozen files in codeable form (e.g. the ЕГСД/ГОСТ lineage named in `STANDARDS.md` and `diversity.md`, ГОСТ referenced across nine `cases/` files, colonial/successor statements in `diversity.md` §10–13) — verified before the analysis is committed, and if edge density is too low the idea reports that as its result rather than inventing edges.
- *assumption:* edge coding rules (what counts as strong descent: same ancestor standard vs influence/translation vs colonial administrative inheritance) are written and frozen before any statistic is computed, with strong/weak edge sets pre-registered as a sensitivity pair.
- *assumption:* with 28 systems and perhaps 4–6 codeable lineage blocks, inference is permutation-only; no asymptotic claim is made.
- *assumption:* genre/declension labels and the slot matrix are used exactly as frozen; no re-coding of outcomes.

**Predicted observations.**
- *prediction:* raw pairwise Jaccard similarity is negatively associated with descent distance (post-Soviet/Germanic/Sinosphere/Francophone clusters are more similar than cross-cluster pairs).
- *prediction:* after blocking on lineage, the S6⊥S9 declension effect survives — genre furniture is re-invented where lines diverge — while part of the universal-stem prevalence attenuates on the subset of systems with no documented shared ancestor.
- *prediction:* the partition is asymmetric: descent explains more of *surface/formula* similarity than of the stem, matching the atlas's own claim that the surface is where traditions diverge.

**Disconfirming observation (prediction).** Disconfirmed if descent distance shows no association with profile similarity and blocked and unblocked nulls agree everywhere — which would strengthen the convergence claim for free and give the atlas a documented answer to the standard "your systems are all related" objection; also disconfirmed if, after blocking, the declension effect collapses, because then "genre, not nation" is itself a descent artefact rather than a headline.

**Main feasibility risk (assumption).** The edge set is likely sparse and asymmetric — lineage statements cluster in the Soviet bloc, Germanic, Sinosphere and Francophone cases (`grep` of `cases/` finds ГОСТ references in 9 files, concentrated in `soviet_bloc.md`), so several systems may end up unblocked and the blocking granularity may collapse to 3–4 usable blocks; single-coder edges add classification error, mitigated only by the pre-registered strong/weak edge sensitivity.

**Repo connections (fact).** `STANDARDS.md` (four-layer inventory, shared-ancestor statements); `diversity.md` §10–13 (tranche, continuity, colonial themes); `cases/*.md` (per-system standard references); `ledger.json`; `analysis/slot_matrix.csv`, `analysis/slot_similarity.csv`, `analysis/qualitative_analysis.md` (implication rules); `analysis/recompute.py`.

**responds to:** E02's located challenge that Galton's problem and area-blocking are prior art for P06-2/P06-3 (adopt that prior art as the instrument instead of re-claiming it); the revise dispositions on P08-3, P07-3 and P05-2 ("freeze coding rules pre-computation"); and the pause on P04-3 (no new standards-edition retrieval needed — edges come from files already on disk). Gap: none of the 48 controls the atlas's convergence claim for common descent.

## P09-3 — Universality as compulsion: does normative force explain the deep grammar?

**Participant: P09 | Stage: post-check | Origin: AI-generated | Date: 2026-10-15**

**Statement (idea).** Use the obligation coding already frozen in `requisites.csv` (150 mandatory / 66 optional / 9 other tokens) as the *explanatory* variable for cross-system slot presence — pre-registered leave-one-system-out prediction of each system's slot vector from the other systems' mandate shares, scored against a prevalence-only baseline — testing whether the universal stem is essentially what every standard compels and whether the epistolary/normative fork is a conflict of genre-conditional mandates.

**Mechanism/rationale (idea).** Every idea at the top of the matrix asks whether a pattern is real (exact nulls, prevalence intervals, tier restrictions, variance partitions) — none asks *why* official documents converge, and E01–E04 repeatedly record "no direct evidence located" on official-document slot morphology, so a mechanism question on frozen data is the safest home for the application-level novelty this round demands. Obligatory force is the only frozen variable that encodes a mechanism, and in the 48 it appears solely as an ablation dose prediction (P02-6) or as a column nobody has yet built (P05-6, who-supplies); no idea makes it the explanation behind findings 1 and 2. The fork itself invites this reading: S6 rows are mandatory in 7 of 11 (S9 in 2 of 3) — "each system compels one declension's furniture and never both" is a normative statement, not a statistical one — while S13 annex is optional in 10 of 14 rows and S7 reference in 7 of 9, predicting that obligation-weighted prevalence reorders the stem.

**Assumptions.**
- *assumption:* the `obligation` column is complete enough to collapse safely; the rare tokens (conditional, emerging, unknown, n/a, supported, mixed — 9 rows) are pre-registered as non-mandatory before anything is computed.
- *assumption:* mandate share (computed on 24 systems) is a genuine predictor of the held-out system's presence/absence, so common-source bias between the two codings attenuates rather than manufactures the effect — this is the point of the leave-one-out scoring, not an afterthought.
- *assumption:* ~25 slot-coded systems × 14 slots gives enough cells for log-loss/AUC comparison with a prevalence baseline, with system-level (not cell-level) resampling for uncertainty.
- *assumption:* obligation status is a property of the *standard*, not of the analyst's view of importance — untestable directly, so reported as a limitation.

**Predicted observations.**
- *prediction:* mandate share improves held-out log loss over the prevalence-only baseline, and the improvement survives declension-blocked resampling.
- *prediction:* obligation-weighted prevalence demotes S13 and S7 (largely optional) while S12/S1/S10 stay on top — universality decomposes into a "compelled" core plus a "merely mentioned" fringe.
- *prediction:* within each declension, furniture requisites are disproportionately mandatory and cross-declension exclusivity holds at the level of *mandates* (no system mandates both S6 and S9), i.e. the fork survives as a normative conflict.

**Disconfirming observation (prediction).** Disconfirmed if mandate share adds nothing over the prevalence baseline in leave-one-out scoring (compulsion explains no variance → universality must be functional or conventional, redirecting effort to the still-unmeasured efficiency claim of `morphology.md` §6); or if optional requisites are held-out-predicted as well as mandatory ones; or if the S6/S9 exclusivity disappears once rows are weighted by obligation, which would show the "formal negation" partly reflects which rows were entered as mandates rather than a structural law.

**Main feasibility risk (assumption).** Common-source bias: obligation and presence were coded together by the same analyst from the same rows, so an apparent association may be partly definitional (leave-one-system-out limits but cannot eliminate this); plus low predictor contrast (150/225 rows mandatory) and slot-level n ≈ 14 for the aggregate test — so exact/permutation inference and effect sizes only, no asymptotic p-values.

**Repo connections (fact).** `requisites.csv` (`obligation`, `slot`, `system`, `genre`); `analysis/slot_matrix.csv`; `analysis/qualitative_analysis.md` (implication rules S6⇒S12, S8⇒S12); `morphology.md` findings 1–2 and §6; `analysis/recompute.py`; `experiment/` (unchanged — no human arm is touched).

**responds to:** gap — the located evidence (E01–E04) found no source bearing on official-document slots and the matrix top (P06-2, P06-4, P06-3, P07-5) is entirely internal "is-it-real" auditing with no mechanism; it also answers the revise dispositions on P05-6, P04-1 and P02-6, which all touch obligation or burden without ever making it explanatory.
