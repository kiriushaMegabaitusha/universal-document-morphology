## P10-1 — Load-bearing-cell adjudication: the week-one gate that decides whether any downstream audit is worth running

**Statement.** Merge P06-2's perfect rules, P06-1's error propagation and P02-1's second-coder
idea into one week-long study that computes, for each headline "perfect" rule, the exact set
of matrix cells whose single flip would break it, re-adjudicates *only* those cells against
the retrieved primary texts in `sources/` under a frozen codebook (LLM used solely to triage
disagreements, never to set a cell), and reports each rule as decidable/undecidable at the
measured cell-error rate — the outcome fixing the scope of P10-2.

**Mechanism/rationale.** Every perfect complementarity/implication rule has flip-distance 1:
one miscoded cell destroys it, so a random-subsample κ (P06-1) spends its budget on cells that
carry no claim. Scoping re-coding to the mechanically enumerable load-bearing set converts
"measure reliability" into "adjudicate exactly what the claims depend on," and comparing the
adjudicated error against each rule's survivable-error threshold yields a per-claim
decidable/undecidable verdict *before* any null model, prevalence interval or tier cut is run.

**Assumptions.** (A1) `sources/` (retrieved texts, ~60 with extracted `.txt`) suffices to
adjudicate most load-bearing cells; cells without primary grounding are flagged unadjudicable,
not guessed. (A2) The LLM is a queue-orderer only — E01's located evidence (LLM annotation
α 0.12–0.46 vs human–human 0.62; downstream conclusions change in >1/3 of cases) rules out any
use as rater or as a bound on human error. (A3) A washout re-code by the sole researcher gives
a test–retest floor distinct from LLM disagreement. (A4) Load-bearing sets are derivable
mechanically from `analysis/slot_matrix.csv` plus the rule statements in `findings.md`.

**Predicted observations.** (P1) Each perfect rule is carried by a small, enumerable cell set
(tens of cells, minutes to compute). (P2) Adjudicated errors concentrate in the thin
secondary-grounded cases, so rules split into "survives on the strong-primary subset" and
"undecidable without re-coding" — reproducing P07-5's tier result at cell granularity rather
than with an arbitrary tier cut. (P3) At least one headline rule flips to undecidable, because
its threshold is 1 flip and load-bearing cells are not error-free.

**What would disconfirm it.** Zero adjudicated errors in every primary-grounded load-bearing
cell, with intervals excluding any non-trivial error rate — then the measurement-error floor is
not the binding constraint, P10-1 collapses into the already-located P07-5 tier restriction,
and it has bought no decision value beyond a cheap week.

**Main feasibility risk.** Sole-coder self-adjudication: with no second human, test–retest after
washout is the only human number and can be inflated by memory of earlier decisions — mitigated
only by adjudicating against source text (external criterion) rather than recall. Second risk:
some load-bearing cells may have no retrievable primary source, leaving those claims
permanently undecidable — to be reported, not resolved.

**Repo connections.** `requisites.csv` · `analysis/slot_matrix.csv` · `analysis/findings.md`
+ `qualitative_analysis.md` (rule statements) · `analysis/recompute.py` (propagation) ·
`analysis/evidence_base.csv` (grounding tiers) · `sources/*.txt` · `ledger.json` (provisional
flags) · `check_claims.py`.

**responds to:** challenge-located on P02-1 (E01: LLM-as-second-coder is mature prior art and

## P10-3 — Cheapest-bin-first sequential falsification of the S6⊥S9 fork across three pre-registered out-of-sample bins

**Statement.** Combine P01-1 (pre-modern instruments), P08-6 (deliberative notes) and P02-2
(document-level scale) into one frozen S6/S9 coding protocol applied *sequentially* to three
out-of-sample bins — bin A: public-domain pre-modern chancery specimens; bin B: openly posted
French/Indian deliberative notes; bin C: ordinary unsorted modern documents — running each bin
only if all cheaper previous bins failed to break the fork, and stopping the study at the first
bin whose co-occurrence rate exceeds a threshold fixed before any coding.

**Mechanism/rationale.** A universal complementarity claim is asymmetrically falsified, so the
information-maximising order is cheapest-falsifying-bin-first rather than three parallel tests:
E01 located *direct* evidence that pre-modern instruments structurally combine addressee and
recital (diplomatics inscriptio+arenga, ferman address+narration, letters patent with whereas
recitals), making bin A a directional prediction with the strongest prior — if bin A cannot
break the fork, bins B–C (which cost more to source) are re-scoped rather than run. One shared
protocol and one scoring rule make the three bins answer P01-1's universality question, P08-6's
third-declension question and P02-2's document-level-vs-aggregated question in a single study
instead of three.

**Assumptions.** (A1) Each bin yields ≥30 licence-clear, openly available specimens within a
week of retrieval; a bin that fails this is dropped, never backfilled with paywalled material
(inherits P04-3's pause logic). (A2) The frozen decision rule separates protocol openings /
basmala-doʿā formulas from normative recitals, pre-empting R01's "vocabulary drift, not a rule
violation" alternative. (A3) Coding is per specimen (document-level unit) — that is precisely
what jointly answers P02-2's aggregation critique. (A4) No human or community data: public
historical and publicly posted documents only; the externally-reviewed boundary-system bins
(P08-1, P08-2) are explicitly excluded pending their review.

**Predicted observations.** (P1) Bin A yields S6+S9 co-occurrence recurring in ≥2 traditions,

document-level co-occurrence rates above the system-aggregated zero, quantifying how much the

## Sequence (why this order maximises information per week)

2. **P10-2, ~1–2 weeks, analytic only.** Outcome decides P10-3: only claims with high
   robustness mass *and* a scored holdout are promoted to an out-of-sample test; flat masses
   mean the fork claim is not yet well-defined enough to falsify externally.
3. **P10-3, ~2+ weeks, retrieval-bound.** Cheap bin A first with early stopping; bin B and the
   expensive bin C each run only if the previous bin failed to falsify. Total: a falsification
   or a scoped survival in ~5 weeks, versus running the 48's top ideas as four independent
   audits in four independent directions.
