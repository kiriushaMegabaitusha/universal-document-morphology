**Direct evidence for the specific test (slot/requisite-level fragility rates across
documentary traditions): none located [none].**

**Status: mixed** — premise supported indirectly; novelty of the audit method challenged
by the significant-properties literature; no direct evidence on the specific test either way.

**Limits.** Snippet-only; two sources seen are commercial blogs (gui.tw, edukatesg.com) and
were not used as authority. Queries were English-language; preservation literature in other
languages not searched. One returned result (arXiv LLM-memory benchmark) was irrelevant and
discarded. Bounded search — non-retrieval is not evidence of novelty.
