# Case: United Arab Emirates — دليل الكتابة باللغة العربية (reference discipline)

*(Resolved: formerly a labelled placeholder. A primary UAE source was retrieved
on the second attempt.)*

- **Instrument:** the *دليل الكتابة باللغة العربية* (Guide to writing in
  Arabic), version 3.0, issued 2022 by the UAE Telecommunications and Digital
  Government Regulatory Authority (TDRA) [S-AE-TDRA, C74].

## What this guide standardises

Its table of contents is unusually telling. It does not list correspondence
slots; it lists **how to name and cite things** [S-AE-TDRA, C74]:

- **اسم الدولة** — the name of the state;
- **رموز الدولة** — the symbols of the state;
- **الشيوخ والصفات الرسمية** — the rulers (Sheikhs) and their **official
  titles**;
- **الجهات الرسمية** — official bodies;
- **الاقتباسات** — citations;
- then **الأرقام، الأيام، الشهور، التوقيت، التاريخ** — numbers, days, months,
  time and dates.

Where the Saudi guide (see `sa.md`) standardises the *correspondence element
set and its rank-conditioned language*, the UAE guide standardises the
**reference apparatus**: the correct form of the state's name and symbols, the
official titles of the rulers, the names of official bodies, and the date/time
conventions. It is a naming-and-citation standard rather than a layout
standard.

## An accidental finding: the Gulf's regional template

Chasing a second Gulf standard (Qatar), a Qatar-hosted legal library was found
to publish a "دليل المراسلات الكتابية النسخة الرسمية". Retrieved, it is
**byte-identical to the Saudi guide** (same MD5) [S-SA-KSU, S-QA-ADLIYAH,
C75]. So the Saudi *دليل المراسلات الكتابية* is not just a Saudi document: it
circulates regionally as a **de facto template**, re-hosted across the Gulf.
That is why this case is one UAE entry and a diffusion finding, rather than a
fabricated separate "Qatari standard".

## The point for the atlas

The UAE shows that "a document standard" can fix a third thing, beyond layout
(Anglophone/Germanic) and language (Francophone): **the reference apparatus** —
who and what you are allowed to call by what name. Together with Saudi Arabia
it also shows a real regional convergence: one guide functioning as the
Gulf's common template.
