# Case: Bénin — the Francophone African document typology

The direct Francophone-African source the Senegal entry lacked: a Béninese
administration course on the document types, plus the Ministry of Economy and
Finance's own procedures manual.

## The document types (Leçon 3, «Les documents de correspondance»)

A Béninese civil-service course sets out the genre system explicitly
[S-BJ-GPE, C76]:

- **La lettre** — the external, formal letter (the "lettre à forme
  administrative").
- **Le bordereau d'envoi** — a *dispatch document* that transmits other
  documents, service-to-service or ministry-to-ministry. It is **a table**:
  the usual letter mentions, the title "Bordereau d'Envoi", and a frame
  divided into three or four columns carrying the text.
- **Le bordereau simplifié, or soit-transmis** — used *only within a single
  administration*: the title **SOIT-TRANSMIS** in capitals in the middle of
  the page, an enumeration of the documents to transmit, and the mention
  **« OBSERVATIONS »** in capitals indicating the object of the transmission.
- **La note** — the internal genre, in three kinds: *note d'information*
  (or *note succincte*), *note de service* (an injunction/instruction), and
  *note verbale* (diplomatic).

## The note drops the formulas

The decisive structural detail [S-BJ-GPE, C77]: the **note** "ne comporte ni
de formule d'appel, ni de formule de politesse". The internal genre carries
**neither the salutation nor the complimentary close** — exactly the two slots
that define the external *lettre*. Within one tradition, the letter is defined
by its formulas and the note is defined by their absence.

## The procedures manual

The Bénin Ministry of Economy and Finance's *manuel de procédures
administratives et techniques* uses a wider type set — **procès-verbal**,
**arrêté**, **décision**, **bordereau de transfert** — and governs the
**imprimés** (printed forms) and **tampons** (stamps), with a *Conseil des
Archives* overseeing records [S-BJ-FIN, C78].

## The point for the atlas

Bénin gives the atlas a **pure case of genre-by-formula**: the
lettre↔note opposition is defined precisely by the presence/absence of the
formules d'appel and de politesse (S6 and S12). It also confirms that the
**bordereau d'envoi / soit-transmis** are real, layout-specified genres — a
dispatch *table* the Anglophone world replaces with a covering email and a
file list.
