# Case: Brazil — Manual de Redação da Presidência da República

- **Instrument:** the Manual de Redação da Presidência da República (3rd
  edition, 2018), the normative style for all federal official
  communications and normative acts, issued under the Casa Civil
  [S-BRAZIL, C34].

## The padrão ofício (standard official format), §5.1

The Manual fixes the parts of an official document in order [S-BRAZIL, C35]:

| § | part | slot |
|---|------|------|
| 5.1.1 | Cabeçalho (header) | S4 / S2 |
| 5.1.2 | Identificação do expediente (type + number) | S2 |
| 5.1.3 | Local e data | S5 |
| 5.1.4 | Endereçamento (addressing) | S6 |
| 5.1.5 | Assunto (subject) | S8 |
| 5.1.6 | Texto do documento | S10 |
| 5.1.7 | Fechos para comunicações (closings) | S12 |
| 5.1.8 | Identificação do signatário | S12 |
| 5.1.9 | Numeração das páginas | structural |

Then §6 lists the **document types**: the *ofício*, *aviso*, *memorando*,
*exposição de motivos*, *mensagem*, and their variations [S-BRAZIL, C36].

## Two distinctive features

1. **An explicit plain-language turn.** The Manual codifies *impessoalidade*
   (impersonality), *clareza*, *objetividade* and *concisão*, and simplifies
   the *fechos* — a deliberate move away from ornate honourifics toward plain
   address. It is, in effect, a national plain-language standard inside a
   document-format standard (compare ISO 24495-1).
2. **Format bound to a digital system.** The *Exposição de Motivos* is tied to
   **Sidof** — the Sistema de Geração e Tramitação de Documentos Oficiais, the
   federal electronic document generation-and-routing system [S-BRAZIL, C37].
   Standardisation and digitisation are fused: the format is the schema of the
   national e-document system.

## The point for the atlas

Brazil is a modern, non-Anglophone, Latin American standard that maps almost
perfectly onto the atlas's slots — but it also shows the two trends the old
European standardisers did not have: **plain language** folded into format,
and **format as the schema of a national electronic document system**. It
sits between ДСТУ (bureau-centric) and Estonia (infrastructure-first).
