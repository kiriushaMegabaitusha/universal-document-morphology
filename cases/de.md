# Case: Germany — DIN 5008:2020

- **Standard:** DIN 5008 (Deutsches Institut für Normung), "Schreib- und
  Gestaltungsregeln für die Text- und Informationsverarbeitung". Revised
  repeatedly since 1949; the 2020 edition no longer refers to the typewriter
  [S-DIN5008].
- **Scope:** writing and layout rules for word processing — one of the
  fundamental standards of German office communication. It fixes **"wie", not
  "was"**: how to lay text out, not what to say.

## The nine components of a business letter

| # | component | carries |
|---|-----------|---------|
| 1 | Briefkopf | sender: company, logo, contact, mandatory company data |
| 2 | Anschriftfeld | recipient address (for a window envelope) |
| 3 | Informationsblock / Bezugszeichen | "Ihr Zeichen", "Ihre Nachricht vom", "Unser Zeichen", phone/e-mail, date |
| 4 | Betreff | bold subject line, without the word "Betreff" |
| 5 | Anrede | salutation |
| 6 | Brieftext | body, left-aligned |
| 7 | Grußformel | closing formula |
| 8 | Unterschrift | company name, signature, printed name/function |
| 9 | Anlagenvermerk | enclosure note |

Two letterhead variants: **Form A** (address field 32 mm from the top) and
**Form B** (50 mm) [C05, C06].

## The interesting contrast

DIN 5008 is a **private-sector** standard: it has essentially no trace/audit
layer (no registration index, no receipt/execution marks). It is the mirror
image of ДСТУ 4163:2020 in the atlas — same universal stem, near-empty S14.
That pairing isolates the audit-layer density variable cleanly.
