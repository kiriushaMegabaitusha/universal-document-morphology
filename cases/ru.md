## Why it is high-value

If the ЕГСД descent is correct, the GOST and ДСТУ requisite sets should be
nearly isomorphic — the same national-standard lineage, diverging only where
the two states diverged after 1991. That would make ГОСТ Р 7.0.97-2016 the
single best natural experiment for the atlas's central claim (that national
document standards differ less than they appear to). It is worth resolving.
