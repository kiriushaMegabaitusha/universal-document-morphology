# Case: Ukraine — ДСТУ 4163:2020

Anchor standard for this atlas (Kyrylo's own jurisdiction).

- **Standard:** ДСТУ 4163:2020 «Уніфікована система організаційно-розпорядчої
  документації. Вимоги до оформлення документів» [S-DSTU2020]. In force from
  2021-09-01, replacing ДСТУ 4163:2003.
- **Companion decree:** Постанова КМУ №55 of 2018 «Деякі питання
  документування управлінської діяльності», approving the Типова інструкція з
  діловодства. ДСТУ 4163 defines the *requisites*; Постанова 55 defines the
  *movement*.

## The 32 requisites (clause 4.1)

01 State Emblem · 02 corporate emblem · 03 superior body name · 04 legal-entity
name · 05 structural-unit name · 06 entity reference data · 07 form code ·
08 entity code (ЄДРПОУ) · 09 document-type name · 10 date · 11 registration
index · 12 reference to the answered document · 13 place of issue ·
14 access-restriction mark · 15 addressee · 16 approval endorsement ·
17 резолюція · 18 control mark · 19 heading to the text · 20 body text ·
21 annex note · 22 signature · 23 seal imprint · 24 visa · 25 endorsement
(agreement/approval) · 26 certified-copy mark · 27 drafter's details ·
28 acquaintance mark · 29 execution mark · 30 receipt mark · 31 state
registration record · 32 copy-medium marker.

## The obligatory minimum (clause 4.4)

A document created by a legal entity **must** carry: 04 (entity name),
09 (document type, not on letters), 10 (date), 11 (registration index),
19 (heading), 20 (body), 22 (signature). Everything else is added per document
type. This seven-requisite set is the smallest legally complete Ukrainian
document — a compact statement of what "a document" minimally is here.

## Notable rules

- A document may have at most **four addressees**; beyond that a distribution
  list is drawn up; the word "копія" is not written before the 2nd–4th
  addressee [C04].
- The **резолюція** (17) is a managerial act written by hand onto the document
  — it is a *dispositif* (S11) embedded in the trace layer.
- All annexes are visé-ed by both the drafter and the head of the creating unit.

## Why this case matters for the atlas

Eight of the thirty-two requisites (17, 18, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 32) serve the
trace/audit slot S14 — the densest audit layer in the sample. It reflects a
complete state recordkeeping regime, not just a formatting convention.
