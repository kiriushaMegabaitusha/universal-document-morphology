# Case: Vietnam — Nghị định 30/2020/NĐ-CP

- **Instrument:** Government Decree No. 30/2020/NĐ-CP on *công tác văn thư*
  (clerical/archival work), which governs drafting, signing and issuing
  documents, document management, file creation and transfer to the archive —
  and, crucially, the **thể thức** (format) and presentation technique of
  administrative documents and their templates [S-VN, C50].
- **Heir of the Sinosphere**: Vietnam belongs to the East Asian *公文* world,
  where a single national format binds the whole administrative apparatus.

## The 14 components of the thể thức

Nghị định 30 details **fourteen components** constituting an administrative
document [S-VN-IMAS, C51]. The core ones:

| # | component | slot |
|---|-----------|------|
| 1 | Quốc hiệu và Tiêu ngữ — national name **and motto** | S4 |
| 2 | Tên cơ quan, tổ chức ban hành — issuing agency | S3 |
| 3 | Số, ký hiệu — number + symbol (agency abbr. + doc type) | S2 |
| 4 | Địa danh và thời gian ban hành — place and time | S5 |
| 5 | Tên loại và trích yếu nội dung — type + subject summary | S8 |
| 6 | Nội dung — body | S10 |
| 7 | Chức vụ, họ tên và chữ ký — position, name, signature | S12 |
| 8 | Dấu, chữ ký số — seal / **digital signature** | S12 |
| 9 | Nơi nhận — recipients (circulation list) | S7 |
| 10 | Other components (contact info, drafting symbol) | S14 |

## Two distinctive features

1. **The state motto is a document slot.** *Quốc hiệu và Tiêu ngữ* places the
   country's full constitutional name and its motto ("Independence – Freedom –
   Happiness") at the head of every document. No European standard has this:
   the document recites the constitutional self-description of the state. It
   is an S4 authority-mark that is *discursive* rather than graphic.
2. **An identifier grammar.** *Số, ký hiệu* composes a number from an
   abbreviation of the issuing agency plus the document type — structurally
   the same idea as the UN symbol (a decomposed, meaningful identifier),
   arrived at independently.
