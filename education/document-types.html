How to read a page map

Each map is one document type. Bands run top to bottom in the order the standard gives its elements; colour is the slot family; the tag on the right is the obligation the standard attaches to that element.

Click a family to isolate it across all six maps. Hover or focus a band for its clause number, its original wording and its slot. must = mandatory, cond = conditional, opt = optional. A dashed band is a slot the type does not carry. On a narrow screen each map scrolls sideways rather than shrinking its labels.

What the six maps show together

  • No slot is common to all six. The nearest are 2 slots at five of six: S12-authentication (missing from the UN resolution); S13-annex (missing from the UN resolution). This is the type-level echo of the atlas's v1.4 regrade, where no stem slot survived at lineage level either.
  • The letter loads the routing block. DIN 5008 puts 4 of its 9 elements in S6–S8; the EU act puts 0 of 6.
  • The act loads the setting and the dispositif. The EU act puts 3 of 6 elements in S9–S11, the UN resolution 2 of 5; DIN 5008 puts 1 of 9.
  • The trace layer is uneven. ДСТУ 4163 spends 8 of 31 elements on S14 alone; DIN 5008, the UN resolution, the EU act carry none.
  • Genre, not nation, moves the slots. Ukraine's own order and letter differ by one requisite, while the Ukrainian and German letters differ by 22 elements.

What these maps are not

  • The maps follow requisites.csv, not the slot profiles in cases/*.md. The two disagree on 22 slot-level judgements across these five systems. Under the case profiles 9 slots would be present in all six types; under the coding table none is. The most consequential is authentication: UN marks S12 present in its case profile while its coded rows carry none, and that single gap is what makes the intersection empty. Where they disagree the map follows the coding table, because that is the table the repository's gates validate.
  • Not measured geometry. Band order is the standard's clause order, not a position measured on a page. No page was measured for this page.
  • Not proportional height. Every band is the same height; a document with 31 elements is not drawn 31 times taller than one with 5.
  • The Ukrainian letter is a derivation. The atlas codes one Ukrainian form. The letter map removes requisite 09 (clause 4.4: the document-type name is not written on letters) and keeps 15, which the atlas codes for correspondence. It is not a separately coded case.
  • The Chinese letter variant is not drawn. GB/T 9704-2012 gives 信函格式, 命令/令格式 and 纪要格式 their own layouts; the atlas codes the general document, so that is what the map shows.
  • The UN symbol's position is an assumption. The coded source fixes the symbol's grammar, not where it sits; it is drawn at the head because that is where a UN document carries it.
  • Not a sample. Six types from five systems, chosen to teach the contrast. The atlas's 28 systems are a convenience frame, and these six are a convenience frame inside it.
