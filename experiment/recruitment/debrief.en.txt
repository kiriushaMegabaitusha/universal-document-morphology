Thank you for taking part! Now that you have finished, here is what it was about.

What was being tested. I am testing the claim that official documents are written
for legal validity rather than for understanding. So every obligation in the study
was presented in two registers:

- formal - in the style of the Ukrainian state standard (DSTU/nakaz) and of EU and
  UN acts;
- plain - following ISO 24495-1, the plain-language standard (short sentences,
  active voice, direct address).

The content is identical in both versions: the same actors, deadlines, numbers and
conditions. Only the way it is expressed changes. You read each obligation in both
registers, in random order, and answered the same questions.

What I measure. The share of correct answers in the formal and plain versions. If
the gap reaches 10 percentage points or more, that supports the claim that formal
register genuinely impedes understanding. If the data exclude such a gap, that is
a result too: it would mean register is not what matters here. The analysis plan
was fixed in advance, before any data was collected, so that the conclusion could
not be fitted to the result.

What happens next. The results feed into the "Universal document morphology"
project (an atlas of official-document standards). Happy to send you a summary
when it is done.

2. If you have already sent the file but would like to withdraw, write to me and I
   will delete your file. It carries only a random identifier, so I can find it
   from what you sent.
