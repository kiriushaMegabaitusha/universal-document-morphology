Invitation to take part in a short study (15-20 minutes)

Hello!

I am studying how people read official documents - part of a comparative atlas of
official-document standards. I am looking for participants and would be very
grateful for your help.

What it involves (15-20 minutes):

1. Open human_survey.html in a browser (double-clicking is enough; no internet
   needed and nothing to install).
2. Choose a language - Ukrainian or English, whichever you prefer.
3. Read 12 short passages of official documents and answer 36 multiple-choice
   questions (three per passage).
4. At the end press "Download results (JSON)" and send that file back to me.

Some passages are close in content - that is expected. Please treat each one on its
own and answer only from the passage in front of you.

Please note:

- Participation is voluntary and anonymous: no name is needed, and nothing is sent
  anywhere automatically - your answers stay in your browser until you download
  the file.
- There is no time limit and no score for speed.
- It is best to finish in one sitting. If you close the page by accident, reopen
  the file from the same place and your progress should still be there.
- Please do not discuss the task content with others until I have closed data
  collection: participants read different versions of the same documents, and
  discussion spoils the measurement.
- You are free to withdraw: if you change your mind after finishing, simply do not
  send the file.

There are two kinds of question: some answers are stated directly in the text,
others require putting two facts together. If you are unsure, pick the closest
option or press "skip".

If you have any questions, or something will not open, write to me and I will help.

Thank you!
